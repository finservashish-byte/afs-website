## Loans Against Securities

Get liquidity from your existing investments — without selling them — via our verified lending partner.

### Loan Against Mutual Funds (LAMF)

Pledge your mutual fund units as collateral and access an overdraft facility. Your SIPs continue, your investments stay in your name.

- No foreclosure charges in most cases
- Interest charged only on amount used
- Quick processing via digital KYC

### Loan Against Shares (LAS)

Similar facility for equity shares held in your Demat account.

### Important Disclosures

- AFS acts as a facilitator and not as a lender.
- The lending partner is not named on this website — available on request.
- Interest rates and loan-to-value ratios are determined by the lending partner.
- Eligibility subject to lending partner's underwriting criteria.

Connect with AFS on WhatsApp for eligibility check and to get started.
