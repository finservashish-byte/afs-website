## Insurance via AFS

AFS facilitates insurance distribution as an IRDAI POSP partner (POSP ID: IP240588) via PolicyBazaar — giving you access to policies from multiple insurers with no bias towards any single insurer.

### What We Facilitate

- **Term Life Insurance** — High cover, low cost. 10x annual income recommended.
- **Health Insurance** — Individual and family floater plans from multiple insurers.
- **Motor Insurance** — Comprehensive and third-party coverage, renewal support.
- **ULIP** — Unit Linked Insurance Plans where investment and insurance combine.
- **Claims Support** — We guide you through the claims process. Not a claims handler, but a support partner.

### Important Note

AFS does not name specific insurers on this website. All policies are facilitated via pan-insurer access through PolicyBazaar POSP. For claim forms and insurer customer care, WhatsApp AFS for guidance.
