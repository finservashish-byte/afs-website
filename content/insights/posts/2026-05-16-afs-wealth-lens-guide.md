## Step 1 — Get Your CAS PDF

Before you can use the tool, you need your Consolidated Account Statement.

**From CAMS:**
1. Go to [camsonline.com](https://www.camsonline.com)
2. Click **Statements → Consolidated Account Statement**
3. Choose **Detailed** (not Summary)
4. Enter your email and PAN
5. Select date range: **Since Inception** (for complete history) or **Last 1 year** (for recent returns)
6. You'll receive the PDF in your email within 2 minutes

**From KFintech:**
1. Go to [kfintech.com](https://www.kfintech.com)
2. Click **Investor Services → Account Statement**
3. Same process — enter email, PAN, get PDF by email

**Important:** Download the **password-protected** version (it's the default). The password is usually your PAN number in uppercase, or your registered mobile number. AFS Wealth Lens supports password-protected PDFs — you'll be prompted to enter the password after uploading.

## Step 3 — Understanding Your Results

Once processed, you'll see four sections:

### 1. Fund-wise Breakup

This shows every fund in your portfolio with:

| Column | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Fund Name** | Scheme name as in CAMS/KFintech |
| **Category** | Large Cap, Mid Cap, Flexi Cap, Debt, etc. |
| **Invested** | Total amount put in (all SIPs + lumpsum) |
| **Current Value** | Today's value |
| **XIRR** | Your actual annualised return |
| **Verdict** | One-line assessment |

**What to look for:** Any fund with XIRR below 8% for equity deserves a review. If it's underperforming its own category benchmark, it may be time to switch.

### 2. Overlap Analysis

This is the section most investors find most surprising.

If you have 4 equity mutual funds, you might assume you're diversified. But if 3 of them are Large Cap funds, they're probably holding 60–75% of the same stocks.

The overlap analysis shows you:
- Which fund pairs have high overlap (above 60% = red flag)
- Your effective number of unique stocks across all funds
- Whether adding a new fund actually adds diversification

**Common finding:** Investors holding Nifty 50 Index Fund + Large Cap Fund + Multi Cap Fund often have 65–75% overlap in the top 30 stocks.

### 3. Estimated Tax Liability

This estimates your **LTCG and STCG** if you were to redeem everything today.

For FY 2025-26:
- **LTCG** (equity held >1 year): 12.5% on gains above ₹1.25 lakh
- **STCG** (equity held <1 year): 20%
- **Debt LTCG/STCG**: As per income slab

This is an estimate — consult a tax professional for exact calculations. But it gives you a useful picture before you make redemption decisions.

### 4. Portfolio Score (out of 10)

The score is based on:
- XIRR vs benchmark (40%)
- Diversification quality — categories, not just fund count (30%)
- Overlap percentage (20%)
- Consistency of SIP (10%)

A score of 7+ is good. Below 5 means your portfolio needs a conversation.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is my data safe?**
Yes. Your PDF is processed entirely in your browser using PDF.js. Nothing is sent to any server. We have no backend database storing your information.

**Which PDF formats work?**
CAMS Detailed CAS, KFintech CAS, and MFCentral statements. Password-protected versions work too.

**What if my XIRR is very low or shows an error?**
This can happen if your statement has very recent transactions (less than 30 days old) or if the date format in an older statement doesn't match. Try with a statement that includes at least 90 days of history.

**Can I use it on mobile?**
Yes. AFS Wealth Lens is fully mobile-responsive. Upload from your phone's files or Google Drive.
