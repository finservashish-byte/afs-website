## Referral Programme

Help someone you know start their investment journey — and earn recognition from AFS.

### Three Tiers

1. **Refer 1 active member** — Public recognition on AFS WhatsApp Channel
2. **Refer 3 active members** — Monthly Digest premium upgrade (extended edition)
3. **Refer 5 active members** — "AFS Champion" status + annual one-on-one review call

To refer, simply share AFS's WhatsApp link or website with someone who would benefit. When they onboard as an active AFS member, let us know — we track and acknowledge every referral.

Finance professionals, MBAs, CAs, or anyone with a strong network who wants to build a supplementary income stream in financial services.
