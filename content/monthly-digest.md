## What You Get Every Month

The AFS Monthly Digest is delivered between the 1st and 10th of every month — to your inbox or WhatsApp.

- 📊 **Market snapshot** — Nifty, Sensex, sector performance at a glance
- 💰 **Top performing funds** — across categories, with XIRR data
- 📰 **Key news decoded** — RBI, SEBI, budget changes explained simply
- 🎯 **Action item** — one thing you should do this month for your portfolio
- 🔍 **AFS Insight** — our honest take on what matters and what doesn't

## Who Gets It?

The Digest is free for all active AFS members. If you're not yet an AFS member, connect with us on WhatsApp to get started — it takes one conversation.
