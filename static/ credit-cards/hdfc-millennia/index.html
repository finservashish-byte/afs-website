Honest Assessment

Who should get this card — and who should not

The only honest way to recommend is to also tell you who should look elsewhere.

✓ Get this card if you…
  • Spend ₹10,000 or more on Amazon or Flipkart monthly
  • Order from Swiggy or Zomato regularly
  • Want platform-specific rewards without complexity
  • Already an HDFC Bank customer — easier approval
  • Need lounge access on a mid-range card
✗ Skip this card if you…
  • Primarily offline spenders
  • Heavy international travellers — Scapia is better
  • Want universal cashback across all platforms — SBI Cashback is better
  • Looking for premium travel perks
