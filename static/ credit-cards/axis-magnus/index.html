Honest Assessment

Who should get this card — and who should not

The only honest way to recommend is to also tell you who should look elsewhere.

✓ Get this card if you…
  • Spend ₹1 lakh or more monthly on the card
  • Travel domestically 4 or more times a year
  • Make international trips at least twice a year
  • Want the best rewards rate available in India
  • Understand and enjoy rewards optimisation
✗ Skip this card if you…
  • Spending under ₹50,000 per month — fee won't justify
  • Want simple cashback without tracking miles
  • First-time credit card users
  • Primarily offline spenders
