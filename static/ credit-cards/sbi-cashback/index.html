Honest Assessment

Who should get this card — and who should not

The only honest way to recommend is to also tell you who should look elsewhere.

✓ Get this card if you…
  • Spend ₹15,000+ online every month
  • Shop on Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho regularly
  • Pay utility bills and subscriptions online
  • Order food delivery frequently
  • Want cashback without managing reward points
✗ Skip this card if you…
  • Spend primarily in cash or offline
  • Want airport lounge access — this card has none
  • Already have HDFC Millennia (very similar)
  • Want travel miles or hotel points
  • Spend under ₹10,000 per month online
