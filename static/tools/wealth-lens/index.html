📊 About the Calculations

XIRR uses Newton-Raphson method on transaction cash flows from your statement. Tax estimates use FY 2025-26 rates: STCG equity 20%, LTCG equity 12.5% above ₹1.25L exemption, debt funds taxed as per income slab (post April 2023 amendment). Overlap analysis is category-based — actual stock-level overlap may differ. All outputs are indicative estimates.
